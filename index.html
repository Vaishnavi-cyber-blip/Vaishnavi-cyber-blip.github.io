About Me

I graduated from JSS Academy of Technical Education with a Bachelor of Technology in Civil Engineering. After that, I joined the ICTD Lab in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at IIT Delhi as a Research Associate on the CoRE Stack project, under the supervision of my advisor, Prof. Aaditeshwar Seth. I am currently a Research Collaborator with the AHL Lab at the National University of Singapore (NUS), working with Dinithi Dissanayake.

My research lies at the intersection of human-centered AI, geospatial computing, and AI for social and environmental impact. I am particularly interested in how computational systems behave when deployed outside controlled settings — where data are incomplete, users differ in digital familiarity, and local or infrastructural constraints challenge the assumptions made during system design. I want to build and evaluate systems that are interpretable, context-aware, and useful to the people making real-world decisions.

At present, my main work is with the AHL Lab, where I analyse Garmin wearable data (heart rate, HRV, activity, and sleep) to derive validated metrics for stress and affect inference, as part of a broader effort on wearable and smart-glasses applications for accessibility and social good. Earlier, on the CoRE Stack project (write-up), I built geospatial decision-support for water-harvesting site suitability using Google Earth Engine and QGIS. Outside research, I volunteered with the Slum Swaraj Foundation and write about machine learning and AI on Medium.

I am interested in PhD opportunities and research collaborations in human-centered AI, AI evaluation, and computational approaches to social and environmental problems.

Geospatial Decision-Support for Water-Harvesting Site Suitability

Research Associate, ICTD Lab, IIT Delhi · advisor: Prof. Aaditeshwar Seth
  • Developed interpretable suitability criteria (slope, catchment area, drainage proximity, stream order, land-use/land-cover) across 24 irrigation-structure types to evaluate where water-harvesting interventions are environmentally suitable.
  • Built a proof-of-concept workflow and an automated site-suitability engine to support evidence-based planning with government and NGO partners.
  • Validated 30 field project reports, characterising failure modes arising from inaccurate land-use classification, missing spatial layers, field-location errors, and mismatches between mapped suitability and practitioner demand.

Gates Foundation ICO AI Fellows Program (Agriculture track)
